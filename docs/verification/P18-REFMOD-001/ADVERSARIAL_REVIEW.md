# P18-REFMOD-001 — Independent adversarial review

**Reviewer:** a fresh subagent with no part in writing the suite, given the *code and the stated rules* (not my conclusions), 22 attack questions, read-only access and the already-built binaries (it ran the 44-case suite, the example runner and a few CLI commands; it did not build). 55 tool calls, 13.4 minutes. Its report is paraphrased below with the disposition of every finding. **0 critical, 4 HIGH, 1 MEDIUM-HIGH, 8 MEDIUM, 3 MEDIUM-LOW, 1 LOW (grouped).** The two most valuable results were findings 1 (several production defects would have passed) and 2 (a documentation claim that was false); both led to code and document changes, not to a note.

Dispositions: **FIXED** = code/test/doc changed and re-verified; **PARTLY** = changed in part, remainder recorded; **RECORDED** = a limit written into the evidence, not changed.
